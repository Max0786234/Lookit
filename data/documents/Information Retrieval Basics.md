# Information Retrieval Basics

Information retrieval is the science of organizing and retrieving information. At its core, IR systems help users find relevant documents from large collections.

## Key Components

### Indexing
The process of organizing documents into an efficient data structure. Modern search engines use inverted indices, which map terms to the documents containing them.

### Ranking
Ranking algorithms determine which documents are most relevant to a query. Common approaches include:
- Term Frequency (TF)
- TF-IDF (Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency)
- BM25
- PageRank (used by Google)

### Retrieval
The process of finding documents matching a user's query. This includes:
1. Query parsing and tokenization
2. Looking up terms in the index
3. Computing relevance scores
4. Sorting results by relevance

## Why Search Matters

Search is fundamental to information access. Without effective search, finding information in large collections becomes impractical. Search engines power:
- Web browsers
- Email clients
- Document repositories
- E-commerce platforms
- Knowledge bases

The quality of search directly impacts user experience and satisfaction.
